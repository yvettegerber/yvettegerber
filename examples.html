Time is a landscape

10.7.2023

A multi-medium alter to time inspired by physics theories on the dimensions and form of time. A poem, a map, and a print. The map shows beauty of physical changes of a riverbed over time, and was made using ArcGIS to transform DEMs along the Red River in Oklahoma into a Relative Elevation Model by developing a river centerline, applying inverse distance weighted interpolation to compute height above river surfaces, and running raster calculations. I used a wooden block printing method to create the print.
